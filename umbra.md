# Umbral - Company Context Document

## Who We Are

Umbral is a technical services firm specializing in data infrastructure, growth engineering, automation, and AI-powered applications. We solve complex problems around data surfacing, data integrity, lead reactivation, high-quality funnel construction, and internal application prototyping.

## Four Pillars

1. **CDP, Warehousing & Insights** - Customer data platforms, data warehousing, and actionable analytics
2. **Go-To-Market Engineering & Reactivation (Growth)** - Strategy-led pipeline building and reactivation using ringless voicemail, email, and agentic outreach
3. **Automation** - Business process automation across departments
4. **AI Workflows & Applications** - Customer-facing chat and voice apps, internal purpose-built MVP tools, agentic workflows that serve as true operational partners

## Client Profile

- **E-commerce**: Texas Metal Works

## Growth Strategy & Positioning

### Enterprise / Mid-Market
- Lead with technical depth across all four pillars
- Emphasize multi-department AI integration (not just assistant-level, but embedded operational partners)

### SMB Expansion (Planned)

### Growth Services Approach
- Strategy-first: help businesses build and reactivate existing pipelines
- Channels: ringless voicemail, email, multi-touch sequences
- Agentic automation layered on top of every growth strategy
- AI as a transformative partner across multiple departments, not just a bolt-on assistant
